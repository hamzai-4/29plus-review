Choosing your care

What is the difference between Rich and Ultra Rich?

Rich pairs a light gel serum with a soft cream and is best suited to combination to oily skin. Ultra Rich uses an emulsion gel serum and cushiony cream for dry to normal skin. Both sets include the same EYE SERUM.

Can I switch between the two ranges?

Yes. Choose the texture that feels comfortable for your skin now. Your preference may change with weather, travel or your routine. Introduce a different formula gradually and patch test first.

Is this skincare for women and men?

The skincare can be used by women and men. Choose by skin needs and texture preference.

What does 29 mean?

The name is inspired by changes in skin from the mid-20s onwards. It is a reminder that care can evolve with you, rather than a fixed biological deadline.

Daily ritual

What order should I use the products in?

On cleansed skin: EYE SERUM, face serum, then face cream. Use one pump of each, morning and evening. Use a separate sunscreen during the day.

Can I use the products under makeup?

The skincare is designed to fit a daily routine and has brand-published feedback on makeup compatibility. Allow each layer to absorb before applying makeup.

How quickly will I notice a difference?

Experiences vary. In the brand’s published participant feedback, 85% using Rich and 75% using Ultra Rich felt more hydrated after 14 days. These are percentages of participants, not percentages of improvement.

Do the creams replace sunscreen?

No. Neither cream is presented as an SPF product. Keep a separate sunscreen in your daytime routine.

Ingredients & suitability

Is the skincare vegan?

The five skincare formulas and their corresponding refills are described as vegan friendly. This statement does not apply to INNER+ collagen or the Gua Sha tool.

Are the formulas fragrance and colorant free?

The topical skincare formulas are described as fragrance free and colorant free. Full ingredient lists are available on the product pages; the supplement is a separate formulation.

What if my skin is sensitive?

Patch test before use. If irritation occurs, stop use. If you have a diagnosed skin condition or are receiving treatment, ask your clinician whether the specific ingredient list suits your care.

Can I use the skincare after laser, fillers or another procedure?

Follow the aftercare advice of the clinician who performed the procedure. Timing and suitability depend on the procedure and your skin; the products are not a substitute for medical aftercare.

INNER+

How do I take INNER+?

Take one sachet daily. Follow your pack for duration and breaks. INNER+ is for adults aged 18 and over. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

What is in a daily serving?

One sachet provides 2.5 g Verisol® collagen peptides, 80 mg vitamin C, 50 mg sodium hyaluronate (including 46 mg hyaluronic acid), 5 mg resveratrol, 5 mg zinc, 0.5 mg copper and 50 µg biotin.

Does INNER+ help hair growth?

Zinc and biotin contribute to the maintenance of normal hair. INNER+ is not presented here as a hair-regrowth treatment, and individual results vary.

Can anyone take INNER+?

It is intended for adults. Avoid it if you have known hypersensitivity to an ingredient. If pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or managing a medical condition, check with a qualified clinician before use. Supplements do not replace a balanced diet.

Refills

Can I buy a refill as my first product?

A refill needs the matching original 29 PLUS bottle and pump. Choose the full product or full set first if you do not already own those.

Which refill goes with my bottle?

Match both the product and the range: RICH SERUM with RICH SERUM Refill, for example. EYE SERUM has one matching refill used in both ranges. Every product page links to its compatible counterpart.

What do I keep when refilling?

Retain the original outer bottle, cap and pump. Replace the inner capsule with its matching refill. Watch the refill demonstration before your first change.

Orders

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is available in the UAE and selected international destinations. International delivery is estimated at 5–8 working days. Available destinations, delivery fees and the final total are confirmed at checkout.

What if my item is defective or incorrect?

Contact hello@29plus.com within seven days of receipt with your order number and clear photos or video. The team will review the issue and arrange a replacement, or a refund if replacement is unavailable. Opened or used cosmetics cannot be returned for a change of mind.

Where do I pay and track my order?

Your selected products transfer to the official 29plus.com store for checkout. Payment, order confirmation and account-based tracking are handled there. Customer care can also help you track an order.
