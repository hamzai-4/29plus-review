The outer ritual

Eye. Serum. Cream.

01

Awaken the eye contour.

On cleansed skin, gently apply one pump of EYE SERUM around the eye contours, avoiding direct contact with the eyes.

02

Layer your face serum.

Tap and massage one pump of Rich or ULTRA RICH SERUM onto your face.

03

Finish with lasting moisture.

Gently massage one pump of the matching cream until absorbed. Follow with a separate sunscreen during the day.
