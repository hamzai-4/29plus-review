Know your formulas

Serum and cream: two steps, different textures.

29 PLUS journal · 23 September 2026

Serum and cream sit beside each other in the 29 PLUS ritual, but they bring different textures to the experience. Getting familiar with both makes the collection easier to shop.

Start with the serum

The face serum sits between EYE SERUM and face cream in the brand’s application sequence. RICH SERUM is described as a thin, bouncy gel; ULTRA RICH SERUM has an emulsion-gel texture. Their individual pages explain the formulas in more detail, including their featured peptide ingredients and complete ingredient lists. A product’s name tells you its place in the range; its description tells you more about the experience.

Meet the matching cream

RICH CREAM has a light, soft texture and includes plant oils alongside its other ingredients. ULTRA RICH CREAM has a more cushioning feel, with ingredients including shea butter and ceramides. The matching cream completes each skincare set. If texture is your main question, comparing those descriptions is a useful place to begin before looking at the finer ingredient details.

A set makes the pairing straightforward

Each complete set contains one EYE SERUM, one face serum and one face cream. Choosing a set keeps the range pairing simple. If you already own part of the ritual, individual bottles let you choose the step you need. Check your shelf first: buying a set and an extra face serum together may give you a duplicate you did not intend to order.

Compare the formula, then the format

Once you have chosen the product, decide whether you need an original bottle or its matching refill. Those are packaging choices within the range. A refill requires the corresponding original bottle and pump; it is not a substitute for buying that packaging on your first order. The product pages link each bottle to its compatible refill.
