Choosing your care

Your first 29 PLUS set: a thoughtful place to begin.

29 PLUS journal · 23 September 2026

Choosing your first skincare set can feel simpler when you separate three questions: which textures appeal to you, what comes in the box, and what you already have at home.

Begin with the feel of the formulas

The two sets offer different face-serum and cream textures. Rich is the lighter range, positioned for combination to oily skin. Ultra Rich is the more cushioning range, positioned for dry to normal skin. These descriptions give you a way into the collection. Read the individual formula details too, especially if there are particular ingredients you already know you want to check.

Know what is included

Both original sets contain EYE SERUM in a 15 ml bottle, face serum in a 30 ml bottle and face cream in a 30 ml bottle. The EYE SERUM is shared across both sets; the face products follow the range you choose. This makes the difference between the sets easier to understand: you are choosing your face textures while keeping the same eye step.

Check your existing routine

A complete set is useful when you want all three steps together. An individual bottle may make more sense if you are replacing just one product. Before adding extras, check what is already included in your chosen set. INNER+ and the Gua Sha tool are separate items in the collection, so consider them as separate choices rather than assuming they are part of the three-step skincare box.

Use the finder as a starting point

Find my ritual asks three questions about how your skin feels, your main priority and how you want to build your routine. It recommends original products, from one essential to complementary steps or a complete ritual. Review the suggested products before adding them to your bag. The finder provides shopping guidance; the product pages remain the place to read the full descriptions, ingredient lists and directions before deciding.
