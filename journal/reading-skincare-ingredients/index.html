Ingredient clarity

How to read a 29 PLUS ingredient page.

29 PLUS journal · 23 September 2026

An ingredient highlight and a full ingredient list answer different questions. Knowing where to look helps you compare 29 PLUS products without expecting one short label to explain the whole formula.

Use the highlights for an introduction

The ingredient section introduces selected ingredients in Rich, Ultra Rich, EYE SERUM and INNER+. Those short explanations are a way to get acquainted with a product. They are not the complete formula. Follow the product links when you want to move from a featured ingredient to the specific bottle or sachet that contains it.

Open the full composition

On a product page, expand Full ingredients & composition to see the published list. On set pages, the lists are separated by product so you can distinguish EYE SERUM from the face serum and cream. Keep that distinction in mind when comparing ranges: an ingredient featured in one formula should not be assumed to appear in every product carrying the same brand name.

Read texture and directions alongside ingredients

An ingredient list is only one part of choosing a product. The page also describes the texture, intended place in the ritual and directions for use. Read those sections together to understand what you are buying. Two products can mention some of the same ingredients while still belonging to different steps or offering different textures.

Keep the packaging as your reference

The website provides a convenient place to explore the collection before ordering. When your product arrives, check its packaging for the latest ingredient and use information. If your question concerns a particular ingredient or a detail you cannot find, contact the team with the exact product name. A specific question about a specific formula is easier to answer than a question about the whole range.
