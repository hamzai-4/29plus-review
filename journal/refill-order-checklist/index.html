Refill thoughtfully

Before you order a refill, check these four details.

29 PLUS journal · 23 September 2026

A refill order should be a simple repeat purchase. A few details on your existing bottle can help you find the right capsule and avoid ordering packaging or formulas you do not need.

Match the full product name

Start with the name on the bottle, including the range. RICH SERUM and ULTRA RICH SERUM are different products with their own matching refills. The same distinction applies to the creams. EYE SERUM has one matching refill used with either skincare ritual. Looking at the complete name is more reliable than choosing by bottle colour or remembering only the word “serum”.

Check that you have the original packaging

The individual refill is a replacement capsule. The original outer bottle and pump are not included, so keep them when your product runs low. If this is your first purchase of that product, choose the original bottle instead. Each refill product page includes a link to the corresponding original, making it easy to switch formats before you add anything to your bag.

Choose individual capsules or a refill set

If only one step needs replenishing, choose its individual refill. A refill set groups the matching eye, face-serum and face-cream capsules together. Compare that with what is actually running low at home. There is no need to reorder every step at the same time simply because you originally bought the products as a set.

Review the bag before checkout

Confirm the product names, quantities and format in your bag. Then use the refill demonstration for the replacement process when your order arrives. Keeping the outer packaging is the purpose of the refill system, but the capsule is still packaging of its own. It is a way to reuse the bottle and pump you already have, rather than a packaging-free purchase.
