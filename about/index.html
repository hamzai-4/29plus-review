Meet Dolores Shelleh.

Dolores’s experience in extreme environments prompted a deeper interest in how skin responds to daily demands. That curiosity led to research, formulation and the creation of 29 PLUS.

Born in the heart of Dubai, 29 PLUS connects peptide-powered skincare with daily skin nutrition, making consistent care part of everyday life.

The meaning behind 29

Care evolves.
So do we.

People may begin to notice changes in their skin from their mid-20s onwards. The name is a reminder to pay attention to your skin’s changing needs.

Care can begin before visible changes appear. Start early with consistent hydration and daily sun protection, then adapt your routine as your skin’s needs evolve.
