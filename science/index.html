QACSLABORATORIES

The laboratory behind the testing.

29 PLUS attributes its clinical testing to QACS Laboratories and independent volunteer panels. Below are the four testing areas published by the brand.

Published testing overview ↗
01Skin tolerance

Dermatologically tested

Assessment of how skin tolerates a formula, including signs of irritation or sensitivity.

How this is assessed

QACS describes dermatologist-supervised patch and repeat-patch testing to assess skin tolerance.

QACS testing methods ↗
02Hydration & texture

Anti-wrinkle & moisture tested

Evaluation of moisturization, skin texture and the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles.

How this is assessed

QACS describes instrumental moisture measurements, clinical wrinkle assessments, photographs and volunteer questionnaires.

QACS testing methods ↗
03Skin microbiome

Microbiome-friendly tested

Assessment of compatibility with the microorganisms naturally present on the skin.

How this is assessed

QACS offers microbiome-friendly and microbiome-balance studies, with protocols tailored to investigate product interactions with skin microorganisms.

QACS testing methods ↗
04Eye-area tolerance

Ophthalmologically tested

Assessment of tolerance around the eye area, relevant to the Eye Serum step.

How this is assessed

QACS describes assessment after a period of product use, including examination with a slit lamp under ophthalmological supervision.

QACS testing methods ↗

Method explanations describe QACS’s general testing services. The exact protocols, participant counts and product-by-product reports for 29 PLUS are not publicly provided.

Laboratory credentials

QACS lists ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation for laboratory competence and ISO 9001 certification for quality management. These apply to the laboratory and its defined scope, not to 29 PLUS products.

QACS quality credentials ↗

Our skincare standards

Our five topical formulas and their matching refills are vegan friendly, fragrance free and colorant free. These are formula statements; product-specific certification documents are not publicly provided.

Certificate status & product scope

The public ISO certificate copies reviewed have validity dates that have passed; current renewal status is unverified. Laboratory credentials do not confirm which methods were used in a particular 29 PLUS study.

Skincare testing and formula statements do not extend to INNER or the Gua Sha tool. A product certification would need an issuing body, certificate and covered products.

What these percentages mean

Ingredients with a role

More than a name
on the label.

Peptides

Rich Serum features Snap-8™; Ultra Rich Serum features Argireline™ and Matrixyl® Synthe’6®; Eye Serum features Haloxyl™ peptides. These ingredients support the formulas’ intended cosmetic benefits.

Hyaluronic acid & hydration

Sodium hyaluronate appears across the skincare range as a hydrating ingredient. INNER contains 50 mg sodium hyaluronate per sachet, including 46 mg hyaluronic acid.

Ceramides, oils & comfort

Ultra Rich Cream contains ceramides and shea butter for a cushioning finish. Rich Cream uses light plant oils for a softer, lighter texture.

Nutrition from within

INNER contains collagen peptides, vitamin C, zinc, copper, resveratrol and biotin. Zinc and biotin contribute to maintaining normal hair; this is not a hair-regrowth claim.
