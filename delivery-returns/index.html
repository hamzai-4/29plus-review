Delivery & returns

Thoughtful care,
all the way to your door.

Delivery in the UAE

Delivery is complimentary within the UAE. Most areas receive orders in 1–2 working days; remote areas may take 3–5 working days. Same-day delivery is available in non-remote areas for orders placed before 11 AM, subject to the official store’s service conditions.

International delivery

Selected destinations include Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Lebanon, Jordan, Egypt, Iraq, the USA, France and Serbia. International delivery is estimated at 5–8 working days; customs can cause delays. The recipient is responsible for any applicable duties, taxes and import fees.

Confirm your destination, delivery charge and final total at checkout. Tracking details are sent after dispatch; use your official store account or contact customer care for help.

If something is wrong with your order

Report defective or incorrect items within seven days of receiving them. Email connect@29plus.com with your order number and clear photographs or video. The team will review the issue and provide return instructions.

After the returned item is received, a replacement is arranged. If a replacement is unavailable, an approved refund may be issued; allow up to 14 business days for processing. Opened or used cosmetics cannot be returned for a change of mind.
