About the tool

Our Diamond Eye-Roller Gua Sha facial massager is designed to reveal glowing radiant skin. This indispensable beauty companion maximizes the effects of our three-step skincare routine. With every gentle motion, it stimulates facial muscles for better collagen production, nutrient absorption, and increased blood circulation, giving increased lift and sparkle for healthier, firmer-looking skin.

How to use

Before starting, cleanse your skin and apply the 29 PLUS Rich or Ultra Rich product tailored to your skin type.

Gently glide the Diamond Eye-Roller Gua Sha over your face to promote a calming effect and enhance your natural glow.

Material & design

The Gua Sha tool is made from zinc alloy. It is a reusable facial massage tool, not a cosmetic formula.

Tips for your ritual

The tool is made from zinc alloy, which can be refrigerated for a refreshing, cooling sensation.

Performing facial Gua Sha in the morning can help with reducing puffiness in the cheeks and under-eyes, while at night promotes relaxation and better sleep.

Using the facial massage tool with our daily cosmeceutical skincare routine, enhances the desired outcome.

Before using the Gua Sha, consult with your physician if you have any skin concerns or recently had any cosmetic procedures.

Care & suitability

We recommend using a soft towel to wipe the tool before and after every use.
