About this formula

A collagen peptides drinkable supplement that supports your skin from within, helps support your body's natural collagen, hydration, and revealing a natural radiance for a youthful, glowing complexion.

Powered by clinically studied ingredients, it supports skin firmness, elasticity, and moisture retention.

How to use

Take one sachet daily. Follow the directions on your pack for duration and breaks. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

Flavour & daily serving

Non-Allergenic Pineapple Flavor. One drinkable sachet daily. For adults 18+.

Care & suitability

For adults 18+. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Supplements do not replace a balanced diet and healthy lifestyle. Avoid if you have known hypersensitivity to an ingredient. Ask a qualified clinician about suitability if pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication or managing a medical condition. Store in a cool, dry place and check the best-before date.

About the evidence

INNER+ combines collagen peptides with vitamin C, hyaluronic acid, zinc, copper, resveratrol and biotin. Supplement ingredients have their own evidence; skincare-set testing does not apply to this product.
