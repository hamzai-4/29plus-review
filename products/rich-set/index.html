About this formula

Reveal a smoother, more refined complexion with our advanced skin-revitalizing set. Designed in three synergized steps. Infused with scientifically-backed active ingredients and highly concentrated peptides, it helps reduce the look of fine lines and expression wrinkles, smooths and refines texture for a clearer complexion, while supporting skin's natural collagen and moisture. Enriched with plant extracts, including Arabian Cotton inspired by tradition, for a visibly healthier, natural glow. Completed with our eye serum, which helps reduce the look of dark circles and puffiness for a brighter, more refreshed eye contour.

How to use

Start with cleansed skin. Apply one pump of EYE SERUM, then one pump of face serum, followed by one pump of cream. Morning and evening. Use a separate sunscreen during the day.

Care & suitability

For external use only. Patch test on a small area before use. Avoid direct eye contact; rinse thoroughly if contact occurs. Stop use if irritation occurs. Store at room temperature, away from direct heat and sunlight. Keep out of reach of children.
