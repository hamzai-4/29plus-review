Texture & skin needs

Cushiony, soft cream. Dry to normal skin.

Both skincare ranges include the same soft liquid gel Eye Serum. Choose the face serum and cream texture that feels most comfortable for your skin.

Care & suitability

For external use only. Patch test on a small area before use. Avoid direct eye contact; rinse thoroughly if contact occurs. Stop use if irritation occurs. Store at room temperature, away from direct heat and sunlight. Keep out of reach of children.
